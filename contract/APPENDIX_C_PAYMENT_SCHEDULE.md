\# 9. Late Payment



If any installment remains unpaid for more than \*\*15 calendar days\*\* after its due date, the Contractor may, upon giving written notice to the Employer:



\- Suspend ongoing development work;

\- Suspend deployment of new features;

\- Delay project delivery timelines proportionately; or

\- Withhold final delivery until all outstanding amounts have been paid.



Any suspension caused by late payment shall automatically extend the project schedule by the corresponding period of delay and shall not constitute a breach of contract by the Contractor.



\---



\# 10. Additional Work



Any work outside the agreed Scope of Work shall require:



\- A separate quotation;

\- Written approval from the Employer; and

\- A mutually agreed payment schedule before implementation.



No out-of-scope work shall commence until such approval has been received.



\---



\# 11. Taxes



Unless otherwise stated in writing, all prices in this Agreement are exclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT).



If the Employer requires a Tax Invoice and VAT is legally applicable, the Employer agrees to pay the applicable VAT in addition to the Contract Value.

\# 12. Currency
