\# Mingalar Bangkok



Version: 1.0



This Appendix forms an integral part of the Software Development Agreement for the Mingalar Bangkok project.



\---



\# 1. Purpose



This Acceptance Test Appendix defines the inspection, testing, review, and acceptance procedures for the Mingalar Bangkok Project.



Its purpose is to ensure that the delivered system conforms to the agreed Scope of Work before final acceptance.



\---



\# 2. Acceptance Criteria



The Project shall be considered substantially completed when all of the following conditions are met.



\- The agreed Scope of Work has been completed.

\- The platform is accessible online.

\- Core system functions operate normally.

\- No Critical Severity defects remain.

\- The Deliverables specified in Appendix D have been delivered.



Minor UI adjustments, wording changes, browser rendering differences, cosmetic issues, or future enhancement requests shall not prevent acceptance.



\---



\# 3. Testing Period



The Employer shall have



\*\*7 Calendar Days\*\*



from the date of delivery to inspect and test the system.



During this period the Employer may submit written comments or report defects.



If no material defects are reported within this period, the Deliverables shall be deemed accepted.



\---



\# 4. Defect Classification



\## Critical



Examples



\- Website cannot be accessed

\- Login does not function

\- Database failure

\- Data corruption

\- Payment process unavailable (if included)

\- Complete system crash



Status



Must be corrected before Final Acceptance.



\---



\## Major



Examples



\- Important feature not functioning correctly

\- Business workflow interrupted

\- Incorrect calculations

\- API malfunction

\- Upload failure



Status



To be corrected within a reasonable time agreed by both Parties.



\---



\## Minor



Examples



\- Typography

\- Alignment

\- Minor UI inconsistencies

\- Small wording corrections

\- Non-critical display issues



Status



May be corrected after acceptance as part of warranty or maintenance, where applicable.

\# 8. Performance Verification



The Employer may verify:



\- Website loads successfully

\- Navigation functions correctly

\- Forms submit successfully

\- Images display correctly

\- Search operates normally

\- Database connections function correctly



Performance may vary depending on the user's device, browser, internet connection, hosting infrastructure, and third-party services.



\---



\# 9. Security Verification



The Employer may verify



\- HTTPS enabled

\- Login security

\- Authentication

\- Authorization

\- Protected pages

\- File Upload Permissions

\- Input Validation



Penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and formal security audits are outside the scope of this Agreement unless separately agreed in writing.



\---



\# 10. Acceptance Decision



Following completion of the Acceptance Test, the Employer shall indicate one of the following.



☐ Accepted without reservation



☐ Accepted subject to Minor Corrections



☐ Not Accepted



If "Not Accepted" is selected, the Employer shall provide a written list of material defects.



\---



\# 11. Final Acceptance



The Project shall be considered finally accepted when:



\- All Critical Defects have been corrected;

\- The agreed Deliverables have been provided;

\- The Employer has signed this Acceptance Test; or

\- The Employer has used the system in production for more than seven (7) calendar days without submitting written notice of any material non-conformity.
