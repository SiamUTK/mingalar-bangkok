This Appendix forms an integral part of the Software Development Agreement for the Mingalar Bangkok project.



\---



\# 1. Project Overview



Project Name



\*\*Mingalar Bangkok\*\*



Project Description



An AI-First digital platform designed for the Myanmar community living, working, studying, and traveling in Thailand.



The platform combines local business discovery, employment, housing, travel services, community resources, news, and AI-powered assistance into one modern web application.



\---



\# 2. Project Objectives



The Contractor shall design, develop, test, deploy, and deliver a production-ready web platform that enables users to:



\- Discover trusted businesses

\- Search for jobs

\- Find housing

\- Explore local events

\- Read community news

\- Use AI-powered assistance

\- Access travel-related services

\- Learn Thai language

\- Manage their personal accounts

\# 24. Acceptance Criteria



The project shall be considered accepted when



\- The agreed core modules have been completed.

\- The system can be deployed and accessed online.

\- Critical defects preventing normal operation have been resolved.

\- The Deliverables specified in this Appendix have been provided.



Minor visual adjustments, browser-specific rendering differences, non-critical enhancements, performance tuning, content updates, and future feature improvements shall not delay acceptance unless otherwise agreed in writing.

\- Content Writing

Any additional work requested outside this Scope of Work shall be subject to a separate quotation and written approval before implementation.
