\# 9. Acceptance Review



At the completion of each milestone, the Employer may review the completed work.



The Employer shall notify the Contractor of any material issues requiring correction within \*\*7 calendar days\*\* after receiving the milestone for review.



If no material issues are reported within this period, the milestone shall be deemed accepted, and development shall proceed to the next phase.



\---



\# 10. Schedule Adjustment



The Contractor may reasonably adjust the Project Timeline where delays arise from:



\- Late delivery of information or materials by the Employer;

\- Requests for additional features or scope changes;

\- Delays caused by third-party providers;

\- Force Majeure events;

\- Technical issues beyond the Contractor's reasonable control.



Such adjustments shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement.



\---



\# 11. Scope Changes



If the Employer requests additional functionality beyond the agreed Scope of Work, the Contractor shall:



\- Prepare a separate quotation;

\- Estimate the additional implementation time; and

\- Revise the Project Timeline accordingly after written approval.



\---



\# 12. Suspension



The Contractor may suspend development if:



\- Payment is overdue;

\- Required project information is not provided;

\- Employer approval is delayed beyond a reasonable period.



The Project Timeline shall automatically be extended by the duration of the suspension.



\---



\# 13. Early Completion



The Contractor may complete the Project earlier than the estimated completion date.



If the Project is completed ahead of schedule and accepted by the Employer, the final payment shall become due immediately in accordance with the Payment Schedule.



\---



\# 14. Final Project Handover



The Project shall be considered completed when all of the following have occurred:



\- Core Deliverables have been completed.

\- Production deployment has been completed.

\- Administrator access has been provided.

\- Source Code has been transferred (upon full payment).

\- Documentation has been delivered.

\- Final payment has been received.
